# About Briefly

## The Problem

Marketing campaigns start with chaos. A client calls with half-formed ideas, a copywriter drops notes into a Slack thread, someone emails a brand deck — and a week later the campaign manager is trying to reconcile three different versions of "what the campaign is about" before anything can actually be briefed to a creative team. The brief, if one gets written at all, is usually a Word doc assembled manually from scattered inputs that quickly go stale.

The cost is real: creatives go in the wrong direction, revisions multiply, deadlines slip. The root cause is almost always a brief that was incomplete, inconsistent, or never challenged on its gaps before the work began.

## What Briefly Does

Briefly is a structured brief builder that turns rough marketing inputs into a clean, AI-analyzed campaign brief — in minutes, not hours.

You start by pasting in whatever you have: raw meeting notes, a client email, a deck summary, bullet points. The app ingests that context and walks you through a six-step guided wizard — covering objectives, target audience, key messages, channels, deliverables, and timeline — pre-filling each section with content extracted from your notes by Claude AI. You review, edit, and fill in what's missing.

Once your sections are filled in, Briefly runs a **gap analysis**: it reads your entire brief and flags specific missing or underdeveloped areas (e.g., "No success metric defined for the primary objective" or "Audience segment lacks behavioral context"). Each flag comes with a suggested action. You can also trigger an inline **AI refine** on any individual section — the model improves what's there without overwriting your voice.

The finished brief can be exported as a formatted PDF or Markdown file, duplicated as a starting point for a new campaign, and browsed from a searchable dashboard.

## Who It's For

Briefly is built for anyone who regularly writes or assembles marketing briefs:

- **Campaign managers** who need to turn client calls and scattered notes into a structured document their team can actually execute against
- **Freelance strategists and copywriters** who want to arrive at client relationships with a professional, AI-assisted briefing process
- **In-house marketing teams** where briefs are currently living in Google Docs with no consistent structure or quality check

It's not a project management tool or a campaign execution platform — it's the step *before* that: getting alignment on what you're building and why, before any creative work begins.
